About Us

Professional Metal, Wood & Aluminium Engineering Company

Who We Are

MEZAM Engineering SARL is a Cameroonian company dedicated to high-quality metal fabrication, aluminium installation and general carpentry. We design durable structures combining strength, functionality and modern aesthetics.

From custom iron beds to aluminium windows and complex metal constructions, our team delivers reliable workmanship and customer-focused solutions.

Our Services

Complete Metal, Wood & Aluminium Solutions

🔩 Assembly & Welding

Professional metal assembly and precision welding for residential, commercial and industrial structures.

🪵 General Carpentry

Wood and metal carpentry including furniture, doors, frames and customized fabrication.

🏗️ Metal Construction

Design and fabrication of staircases, railings, metal frameworks and structural works.

🪟 Aluminium Cladding

Premium aluminium windows, partitions, cladding and architectural finishing.
